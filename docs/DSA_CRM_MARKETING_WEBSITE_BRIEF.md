# CreditTrust DSA CRM Marketing Website Brief

## Purpose

Build a separate marketing website for CreditTrust DSA CRM. The goal is to sell the CRM as a practical business tool for DSAs, loan agents, channel partners, and finance distributors.

This website should not look like a direct bureau/CIBIL resale portal. The positioning should be:

> A modern DSA CRM for lead management, eligibility intelligence, lender workflow, file tracking, team control, and business reporting.

## Core Positioning

CreditTrust DSA CRM helps loan businesses manage the full journey from lead to file movement:

- Capture and manage leads
- Run customer eligibility checks
- Track follow-ups and files
- Manage lender selection
- Track loan application status
- Manage teams and roles
- Use eligibility credits
- View reports and business performance

Avoid technical or direct bureau-heavy language such as:

- CIBIL resale portal
- Bureau resale system
- Direct CIBIL selling

Prefer softer business language:

- Eligibility intelligence
- Consent-based customer checks
- Credit readiness insights
- Faster customer qualification
- Smarter lender routing
- Lead-to-disbursal workflow

## Target Users

- DSAs
- Loan agents
- Loan consultants
- Channel partners
- Small finance distributors
- Multi-agent loan offices

## Recommended Pages

### 1. Home

Goal: Explain quickly that this is a CRM built for DSA workflows.

Hero angle:

> DSA CRM built for faster loan eligibility, lead tracking, and file movement.

Supporting copy:

> Manage leads, check customer fit, assign files, select lenders, track follow-ups, and grow your loan business from one dashboard.

Sections:

- Hero with product UI/dashboard mockup
- Problem section: scattered leads, manual follow-ups, wrong lender selection, slow qualification
- Solution section: one CRM for leads, eligibility, files, lenders, team, and reports
- Feature highlights
- Trust strip for DSAs, loan consultants, and finance distributors
- Demo CTA

Secondary CTA:

- Eligibility checker
